What kind of note is this?

Why the difference? The official ICD-10-CM coding rules treat them differently. At an office visit, a diagnosis may still be a guess, so it shouldn't go on the patient's record as fact. By discharge, the hospital has usually tested and treated for it.

Which conditions should get codes?

Why the difference? A note often lists old conditions the doctor didn't deal with that day. "Only this visit" gives the codes for what was treated at this visit. "Everything in the note" gives a full list of what the note documents.

Example: the visit is for a cough, and diabetes appears only in the past history. "Only this visit" codes the cough. "Everything in the note" also codes the diabetes.

When you check the codes, judge them by what you chose here. In this example, a diabetes code is wrong under "Only this visit" and right under "Everything in the note".

What the numbers mean
Accuracy
Of the codes the coder suggested, how many you marked correct.
Completeness
Of all the codes the note needed (the correct ones plus the ones you added), how many the coder found.
Overall score
Accuracy and completeness combined into one number. 100% means every code right and none missing.
Notes fully right
Notes where you changed nothing.
Review flags
Of the codes you marked wrong, the share the coder had already flagged for a person to check.
