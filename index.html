    Welcome to the test bench

    Here's how it works:

    1. Add your notes. You can add many at once.
    2. The tool suggests ICD-10 codes for each note.
    3. Check the notes one by one, and tell us which codes are right or wrong.
    4. When you've finished your notes, download your report and email it to Triman.

    To begin, enter your name and the tester key Triman gave you.

    1Add your notes

    Add as many notes as you like, then check them one by one. Use de-identified notes only.
    Drop your note files herePDF or text files (one note in each), or an Excel file (one note in each row). The codes are fetched for you.

    My finished notes

    When you're done, press Download my report and email the file to Triman. Click a note to open it again.

    These notes are saved on this computer, in this browser, until you delete them.
    What the numbers mean
    Accuracy
    Of the codes from the assessment and plan, how many you left as correct. Suggested codes from the rest of the note are counted on their own.
    Completeness
    Of all the codes the note needed (the correct ones plus the ones you added), how many the coder found.
    Overall score
    Accuracy and completeness combined into one number. 100% means every code right and none missing.
    Notes fully right
    Notes where you changed nothing.
    Review flags
    Of the codes you marked wrong, the share the coder had already flagged for a person to check.
