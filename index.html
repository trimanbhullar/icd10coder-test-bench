Welcome to the test bench

Paste a clinical note, let the ICD-10 coder suggest codes, then mark each code right or wrong and add any it missed. Every note you check goes into your test list, which you export as a report and email to Triman.

To start, enter your name and the tester key you were given.

Clinical note

Paste the whole note as text, markdown or HTML. Use de-identified notes only.
Setting Codes for

Test list

Every note you've checked, with the totals for your report. Click a row to open it again.

Your list is kept in this browser until you clear it. Email the downloaded report to Triman.
What the numbers mean
Accuracy
Of the codes the coder suggested, the share you marked correct (precision).
Completeness
Of all the codes the note needed (the correct ones plus the ones you added), the share the coder found (recall).
Overall score
Accuracy and completeness combined into one number (F1). 100% means every code right and none missing.
Notes fully right
Notes where you changed nothing.
Review flags
Of the codes you marked wrong, the share the coder had already flagged for a person to check.
